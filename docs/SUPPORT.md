# Mosaic Support

Need help? Send us a message through [our contact page](https://kitchenlabs-one.vercel.app/contact). We usually reply within two days.

## Common questions

**Which teams can I scout?**
The ten VCT Americas teams in Mosaic's sample: 32 playoff series from Stage 1 2024, Stage 1 2025
and Stage 2 2025. The data is fixed; it doesn't update.

**How do I share a report?**
Press **Copy link**. The link includes the team, filters and tab, and anyone who opens it sees the
same report. Links from Mosaic's hackathon version no longer work; pick the team again.

**What do the numbers mean?**
*Eco*, *force buy* and *full buy* are what the whole team spent when the buy phase ended: under
10,000 credits, 10,000 to 20,000, and 20,000 or more. *KAST* is the share of rounds where a player
got a kill or an assist, survived, or was traded. A *retake* is a defense round after the other
team planted the spike.

**What does "Solid sample", "Small sample" or "Thin sample" mean?**
How much a call rests on: 30 or more rounds is solid, 10 or more small, fewer thin. Weigh thin ones
lightly.

**Why does the analyst say "AI unavailable"?**
The AI service didn't answer (it may be busy or paused), so Mosaic shows the numbers behind your
question instead. Try again in a few minutes. Each visitor can get 20 AI answers an hour.

**Why are there no team logos, agent pictures or spike sites?**
Team logos belong to the teams, so Mosaic uses text badges. Agents and maps are shown by name. The
match feed Mosaic uses has no spike-site or damage data, so it doesn't guess.
